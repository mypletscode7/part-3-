A place to master the MYP

MYP Haven is an all-in-one platform designed to help IB Middle Years Programme students stay organized, build confidence, and achieve their academic goals.

Built to make the MYP effortless

MYP Haven’s objective is to centralize relevant resources, improve academic organization, and help students better understand and achieve MYP expectations . MYP Haven hopes to be that secondary place which helps with organization and understanding.

What’s Inside

Dashboard Home
Gives students a quick overview of their tasks, study schedule, MYP progress, goals, upcoming assessments, and quick actions.
🏠

My Subjects
Allows students to organize their MYP subjects, view subject-related tasks and assessments, access resources, and track progress.
📚
FEATURE 3
Task & Assignment Tracker
Helps students manage assignments, deadlines, priorities, and completion status in one place.
✅
FEATURE 4
Study Scheduler
Helps students plan and organize study sessions using daily schedules and weekly calendars.
🗓️
FEATURE 5
MYP Assessment Tracker
Allows students to track their achievement levels, assessment history, MYP criteria, target levels, and progress.
🎯
FEATURE 6
Goals & Progress
Allows students to set academic and personal goals, establish target grades, and monitor their progress.
📈
FEATURE 7
MYP Resources
Provides students with information and resources to help them understand the MYP and its requirements.
📖
FEATURE 8
Command-Term Database
Helps students understand MYP command terms, what they require, and how to use them in different subjects.
🔎
FEATURE 9
Criterion Self-Assessment
Allows students to check their work against MYP criteria, reflect on their work, and identify areas for improvement.
📝
FEATURE 10
Study & Work Support
Provides students with strategies for studying, managing time, taking notes, revising, avoiding procrastination, and preparing for assessments.
